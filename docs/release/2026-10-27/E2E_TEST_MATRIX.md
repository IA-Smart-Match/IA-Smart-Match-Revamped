## Defects and findings (with evidence)

- **D-01 (doc drift, low).** `docs/operations/exercise-hosting.md` §3 says eight `exercise_*` tables. Migration 0048 added `exercise_team_code`, so the exhaustive check returns nine. Evidence: ENV-05.
- **D-02 (walkthrough, medium).** `tools/walkthrough/README.md` says instructors can open results "before recording". On a fresh database that is impossible: `/v1/exercise/instructor/events` returns 409 `exercise_no_teams_yet` until a team enters. The walkthrough script's first step must come after a team enters. Evidence: I-02.
- **D-03 (script or UI flake, medium, not proven).** Team 1's Round 1 confirm click timed out after the server had recorded the run (201). Team 2 did the same click without timing out. A first-visit product tour ("Tour 1 of 6", bottom-right) sits over the send controls and may intercept clicks; this is suspected, not reproduced. Evidence: screenshots/t1-ERROR.jpg and the 201 count.
- **D-04 (walkthrough script, medium).** `record_student_flow.mjs` selects `Team N` and then clicks "Open this team's work", but the page now also requires the 4-digit team code, and the instructor step is ordered wrong (D-02). The script as written would fail at step 1. Not fixed: the original file is untouched; the adapted copy is `e2e/run_e2e.cjs`.
- **D-05 (compose, low, informational).** `docker-compose.exercise.yml` hard-sets `SMARTMATCH_EXERCISE_COOKIE_SECURE: "true"`, so the local `.env` value `false` is overridden. Flow worked on http://127.0.0.1 because Chromium treats loopback as secure.
- **D-06 (environment).** No product defect. Docker Hub anonymous pulls returned 429, which blocks compose image builds for api-exercise and web-exercise.

## Student-facing UX blockers (observed, not fixed)

1. On a fresh exercise the events page says "Results not open yet" and never says who opens them or how long it takes. Students cannot tell that the instructor must act.
2. The instructor's "Open results" list is hidden until a team enters ("No team has entered a number yet."). A teacher who signs in first sees a dead-end panel.
3. A first-visit tour overlay ("Tour 1 of 6", Skip/Next) appears on entry and on results pages, covering the bottom-right controls.
4. The 4-digit team code is a new field with minimal explanation ("Your instructor has it.") and no check against the chosen tile until submit.
5. Round 2 is locked until Round 1 results run, and the events page says so only in small text ("Harbor opens after your Round 1 results").
