Teaching point — shown at the end

"AI helps a marketer use customer information to decide whom to reach — the marketer still decides what information matters, what message fits, and who is being overlooked."

What this tool is not

  • No real student data, no real emails, no accounts.
  • No machine learning — a transparent weighted sort, tuned by the student.
  • All 300 profiles are fictional.
  • The invite-only speaker workflow is a separate phase-two item.
