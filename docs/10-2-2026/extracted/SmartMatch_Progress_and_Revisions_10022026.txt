1. Every press must show that it worked
Right now it is often hard to tell whether a button did anything. This is the most important fix for class use. (Lead: Janice, with Danny.)
After every press, a message appears next to the button saying what happened. Example: "Saved 'Setting A' for Northline. You have 2 of 3 slots left."
If nothing happened, the message says why. Example: "Results for Harbor are not open yet. Ask your instructor."
A button that can be used only once turns grey afterward and says so. Example: "Results already run for this event."
Each team page has a line at the top that is always visible: team number, which event, results used or not, way of asking chosen or not, refresh done or not.
Changes to a team's saved work show the time. Example: "Refreshed at 10:42 AM."
Buttons that wipe or change work (clear a team, refresh every team at once, switch every team to a new file) ask "Are you sure?" first and say what will change.
